# Pocket Empires — Art Direction & Medieval HUD Craft

## 1. Visual Identity (Golden-Age Isometric RTS)
- **Palette & Materials:** Carved dark oak (`#17120c`), beveled warm castle stone (`#27211b`), hammered brass (`#f5b942`), royal sapphire (`#2563eb`) vs imperial crimson (`#dc2626`) heraldry, and rich biome earth tones.
- **Strict Anti-Slop Bans:** Zero generic white SaaS cards, zero default browser buttons, zero walls of instructional text, zero external runtime image/font fetches (100% Devvit CSP compliant).
- **Isometric Battlefield (`16×16`):**
  - Painterly diamond tiles with light/shadow bevels, animated sparkling water & ford ripples, extruded stone cliffs, multi-canopy swaying forests that thin as harvested, crimson berry bushes, and glinting gold veins.
  - Expressive silhouettes: smoking Town Center chimneys, fluttering banners, scaffolding during construction, villagers visibly carrying wood/gold/berries, armored Knights with plumes, and golden **WOLOLO** conversion beams.
  - 3-state Fog of War + interactive classic Diamond Isometric Minimap.

## 2. Viewport & Mobile-First Ergonomics (`390×844` + `1280×800`)
- **80%+ Unobstructed Battlefield:** Slim 44px Top Stone Resource Ledger + compact Bottom Command Dock.
- **One-Thumb & Classic Mouse Dual Mastery:** Drag-box unit selection + right-click orders on desktop; Smart Squad Chips (`Idle Villager`, `All Army`, `Monks`, `Siege Keep!`) + single-tap smart tile orders on mobile.
